隱私權政策

FinSight – iOS 應用程式 ・ English

本隱私權政策說明 FinSight（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理你的資料。簡單來說：開發者沒有伺服器，也看不到你的帳本內容。

你的理財資料

  • 你輸入的交易、資產、定期收支、財務目標與待買清單，儲存在你的裝置上。
  • 若裝置已登入 iCloud，這些資料會同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有空間（Apple CloudKit），用於在你的裝置之間同步。開發者無法存取。
  • 若你建立或加入共享帳本，該帳本的資料會透過 iCloud 分享給你邀請的成員，成員可看到帳本內容以及彼此的 iCloud 參與者名稱。
  • 類別圖示、顏色、顯示幣別等偏好設定只存在裝置上。

市價與匯率查詢

為了更新市價，本 App 會直接從你的裝置向下列公開資料來源發出查詢。送出的內容只有股票／加密貨幣代碼或你輸入的搜尋關鍵字，不含你的身分、持有數量或金額。這些服務會如同任何網站一樣看到你的 IP 位址。

  • Yahoo Finance（股票、ETF、貴金屬、外匯）
  • CoinGecko（加密貨幣）
  • 臺灣證券交易所（中文名稱搜尋）
  • CNN Fear & Greed、alternative.me（市場情緒指數）

購買

  • 「支持開發者」的年度訂閱，付款完全由 Apple 處理。開發者不會取得你的付款資料。
  • 購買狀態由 Apple 簽章並在你的裝置上驗證，本 App 不會把購買紀錄傳送給開發者或任何第三方。
  • 開發者只能從 Apple 取得無法識別個人的銷售統計報表。

回報問題與 Log

  • 本 App 會在裝置上保存技術性 Log 以便排查問題。Log 只記錄識別碼、筆數與錯誤碼，不含金額、資產名稱或備註內容。
  • 只有在你主動使用「回報問題」並寄出郵件時，Log 才會作為附件離開你的裝置。開發者會因此收到你的電子郵件地址與你填寫的內容，僅用於回覆與修正問題。

我們不做的事

  • 不放廣告，不使用廣告追蹤或分析 SDK。
  • 不要求註冊帳號，不收集姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人或相片。
  • 不販售或分享你的資料。

資料保留與刪除

  • 你可以在「設定 → 清除目前帳本資料」刪除帳本內容；若為共享帳本，雲端與成員裝置上的資料會一併刪除。
  • 你可以隨時退出共享帳本，或由建立者刪除共享。
  • 刪除本 App 會移除裝置上的資料；iCloud 中的資料可在 iOS「設定 → Apple 帳號 → iCloud → 管理帳號儲存空間」中刪除。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

本政策若有更新，會公布於本頁並更新修訂日期。

Privacy Policy

FinSight – iOS Application

This Privacy Policy describes how FinSight ("the app") handles your information. In short: the developer operates no servers and cannot see the contents of your ledgers.

Your Financial Data

  • The transactions, assets, recurring items, goals, and shopping lists you enter are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, this data syncs to your own private iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit) so it is available across your devices. The developer has no access to it.
  • If you create or join a shared ledger, that ledger's data is shared through iCloud with the members you invite. Members can see the ledger's contents and each other's iCloud participant names.
  • Preferences such as category icons, colors, and display currency are stored only on your device.

Market Data and Exchange Rates

To update prices, the app sends requests directly from your device to the public data sources below. These requests contain only ticker symbols or the search terms you type. They do not include your identity, holdings, or amounts. As with any website, these services can see your IP address.

  • Yahoo Finance (stocks, ETFs, precious metals, foreign exchange)
  • CoinGecko (cryptocurrency)
  • Taiwan Stock Exchange (Chinese name search)
  • CNN Fear & Greed and alternative.me (market sentiment index)

Purchases

  • Payments for the optional supporter subscription are processed entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.
  • Purchase status is signed by Apple and verified on your device. The app does not send purchase records to the developer or to any third party.
  • The developer receives only aggregated sales reports from Apple that do not identify individuals.

Issue Reports and Logs

  • The app keeps technical logs on your device for troubleshooting. Logs contain only identifiers, counts, and error codes, and never amounts, asset names, or notes.
  • Logs leave your device only when you choose to send an issue report by email. The developer then receives your email address and the content you wrote, and uses them only to respond and fix the problem.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not show advertisements or use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete a ledger's contents in Settings → Clear current ledger data. For a shared ledger, this also deletes the data in iCloud and on members' devices.
  • You can leave a shared ledger at any time, and its creator can delete the share.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data in iCloud can be deleted in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children's Privacy

FinSight is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
