Privacy Policy

Word Warrior – Mobile Application

This Privacy Policy describes how Word Warrior ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and display name, provided when you sign in with Google or Apple. We use these to create and identify your game account.
  • You may change your display name in the app at any time.

Game Data

  • Game results, scores, experience points, levels, job class, and daily play streaks.
  • Multiplayer match history and competitive (Elo) ratings.
  • Your display name and scores appear on public in-game leaderboards visible to other players.

Purchase Data

  • Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • We keep a record of the in-app items your account owns and a log of purchase events (product, time, platform) in order to deliver your purchases and prevent fraud.
  • Purchases are managed through RevenueCat, which processes purchase receipts on our behalf.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

  • Preferences such as keyboard layout, sound, theme, and language settings are stored locally and never leave your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or photos.
  • We do not serve advertisements or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

Service Providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the service. Each receives only the data necessary for its function:

  • Supabase – backend hosting, authentication, and database (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat – in-app purchase management (privacy policy)
  • Apple – payment processing and sign-in (privacy policy)
  • Google – sign-in (privacy policy)

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app (profile menu → Delete Account). Deletion is immediate and irreversible: your profile, game records, rankings, and purchase log are removed from our servers.

Purchase receipts remain with your Apple ID under Apple's own policies; if you create a new account later, you may restore previously purchased items through the App Store's Restore Purchases mechanism.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with access controls that prevent other users from reading or modifying your data.

Children's Privacy

Word Warrior does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
