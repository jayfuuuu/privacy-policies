Word Warrior – Mobile Application

Need help with Word Warrior? We're happy to assist with any questions about gameplay, your account, or purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • My purchased items are missing. Open the in-app Store and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Items are tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored on the same Apple ID.
  • How do I delete my account? Tap your profile on the home screen, then choose Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Multiplayer won't connect. Online matches require an internet connection. If the service is under maintenance, please try again later.
